### Slide 2: Introduction & Background
*   **Heading:** Introduction & Background
*   **Content:**
    *   **Problem Area:** Managing hundreds of lab equipments (hardware, sensors) in college.
    *   **Importance:** Shifting from manual paper registers to a digital platform prevents loss and optimizes asset usage.
    *   **Literature Highlights:** Existing systems are either too expensive (enterprise tools) or not designed for academic environments.
*   🗣️ **Speaker [Member 5 - Requirement Gatherer]:** "Currently, college labs track expensive equipment using physical paper registers. This manual process is slow and lacks real-time visibility. Our project is a web-based platform designed specifically to digitize this entire inventory and borrowing process for our institution."

---

### Slide 3: Problem Statement
*   **Heading:** Problem Statement
*   **Content:**
    *   **Clear Definition:** The absence of a centralized, accessible digital tool to manage lab equipment borrowing and returns.
    *   **Gaps in Existing Solutions:** Manual logs get lost or damaged. There is no accountability for broken items, no real-time availability checking, and no usage analytics.
*   🗣️ **Speaker [Member 5 - Requirement Gatherer]:** "The main problem is that students don't know if equipment is available before visiting the lab. Paper logs get lost, damaged items go untracked, and teachers have no data on which equipment is used the most."

---

### Slide 4: Objectives
*   **Heading:** Objectives
*   **Content:**
    *   1. Design a role-based web application to digitize lab equipment inventory.
    *   2. Implement automated stock management based on physical issues and returns.
    *   3. Integrate QR code generation for quick physical asset identification.
    *   4. Develop a maintenance module to track damaged/lost equipment.
*   🗣️ **Speaker [Member 4 - QA/Tester]:** "Our main objectives are: First, to build a role-based web app. Second, to automate stock adjustments. Third, to use QR codes for fast scanning. And fourth, to create a strict maintenance system for damaged items."
